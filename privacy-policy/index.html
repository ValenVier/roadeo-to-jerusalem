Privacy policy

App: Road(eo) to Jerusalem

Developer: Disciples (student game development team), Stockholm, Sweden

Contact: Email

Last updated: 15 October 2026

1. Overview

Road(eo) to Jerusalem ("the game") is a mobile racing game made as a school project. We collect as little personal data as possible. This policy explains what data the game may use, why, and what your rights are.

2. Data the game may use

  • Player profile: your player name and your donkey customization choices (fur color, facial expression, hat).
  • Game data: statistics such as races played or won, and your progress.
  • Account data: if you sign in, an account identifier and, depending on the sign-in method, your email address (through Firebase Authentication).
  • Multiplayer data: when you play online, your player name, customization and in-game actions are shared with the other players in the same match. To connect players, our networking provider (Photon) processes technical identifiers such as your IP address.
  • Advertising data: if the game shows ads, the ad provider (Unity Ads) may use your advertising ID and basic device information.
  • Technical data: device model, operating system version and crash or performance information.
  • Purchases: if in-app purchases are offered, they are handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details.

The game does not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, files or microphone audio, and it has no chat or voice features.

3. How we use data

  • To run the game, including online matches
  • To save your profile and progress
  • To show ads and process purchases (if offered)
  • To find and fix bugs and improve performance

4. Third-party services

The game may use the following services, which can process data as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play and Google Play services: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase, by Google: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Unity (including Unity Ads): unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Photon Engine, for online multiplayer

5. Sharing of data

We do not sell your personal data. We only share data with the service providers listed above and, in online matches, with the other players as described in section 2.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data only as long as needed for the purposes above. You can ask us to delete your data at any time by writing to Email. We will answer within 30 days.

7. Children

The game is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Your rights

If you are in the European Union or the European Economic Area, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or move your personal data, and to object to its use. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY). To use your rights, contact us at YOUR_CONTACT_EMAIL.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data, but no system is completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.

11. Contact
