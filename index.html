Road(eo) to Jerusalem

So the last shall be first, and the first last.

Matthew 20:16

Race your donkey through the streets of Jerusalem. Tackle your rivals, steal Jesus from the donkey in front of you and carry him over the finish line.

In development for Android.

See how it plays

Your phone is the steering wheel

No buttons to find. You race by moving the phone. Hover over a phone, or tap it, to see the move.

  • Tilt left and right

    Steer your donkey around corners and away from obstacles.

  • Tilt forward and back

    Lean forward to gallop faster. Lean back to slow down.

  • Shake to tackle

    Catch a rival at close range, shake the phone and knock them off balance.

The last shall be first

Every race has one prize that matters more than your speed: Jesus. One donkey carries him at a time, and any rival close enough can tackle you and take him.

When the race ends, the donkey that carries Jesus climbs up to four places in the final ranking. A donkey in last place can still win, if it holds on to him at the right moment.

Think Mario Kart, with capture the flag. The flag is Jesus.

Items
